V_rot is the mean tangential proper-motion velocity per radial ring (Bianchini et al. 2018 method) — real rotation shows up as a ring-wide offset, not a sinusoid. σ is deconvolved for Gaia measurement errors. The rotation map shows mean v_tan per annular sector: coherent one-color rings = rotation; a red/blue dipole = systemic-PM residual.

Where this cluster sits in the full sample — its bar is highlighted.

Left: rotation strength V/σ, with error bars; the two clusters with no bar (NGC 2419, Pal 5) are unmeasurable — Gaia's errors exceed their internal motions. Right: dynamical age, the number of relaxation times a cluster has lived through (Age / trh); a high value means many close stellar encounters have had time to erase ordered rotation. These two quantities are the strongest correlation in our sample (Spearman ρ = −0.45, p = 0.035): dynamically old clusters rotate less.
